What every clean covers

This is our checklist, room by room. Switch to deep clean to see what we add for a first visit or a move.

Kitchen
  • Counters and backsplash wiped down
  • Sink and faucet scrubbed and shined
  • Stovetop degreased
  • Outside of the fridge, oven, microwave and dishwasher
  • Inside the microwave
  • Table and chairs wiped
  • Floor vacuumed and mopped
  • Inside the oven (deep clean)
  • Inside the fridge (deep clean)
  • Cabinet fronts wiped top to bottom (deep clean)
  • Range hood and filter degreased (deep clean)

Bathrooms
  • Toilet cleaned inside and out, base included
  • Shower, tub and tile scrubbed
  • Sink, counter and mirror cleaned
  • Faucets and fixtures shined
  • Trash emptied
  • Floor mopped
  • Grout scrubbed (deep clean)
  • Soap scum and hard-water spots taken off the glass (deep clean)
  • Cabinet fronts and baseboards wiped (deep clean)
  • Exhaust fan cover dusted (deep clean)

Bedrooms and living areas
  • Every surface we can reach dusted
  • Mirrors and glass cleaned
  • Beds made, sheets changed if you leave fresh ones out
  • Carpets vacuumed, hard floors mopped
  • Trash emptied
  • Baseboards, door frames and light switches (deep clean)
  • Ceiling fans and vents (deep clean)
  • Window sills and tracks (deep clean)
  • Under furniture we can safely move (deep clean)

Starred items are added in a deep clean.
